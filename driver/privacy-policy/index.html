Nitro Driver Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nitro Driver handles information when you use the Nitro mobile application.

1. About Nitro Driver

Nitro Driver helps drivers:

  • Sign in with Google
  • Scan passenger QR codes
  • Create and manage an active journey
  • Configure a journey origin
  • Optimise routes
  • View maps
  • Open external navigation
  • Confirm passenger drop-offs

This policy applies only to the Nitro Driver app. It does not currently cover NITRO-GO.

2. Information Nitro processes

Account information

Nitro currently uses Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication. This may involve:

  • Google account email address
  • Google display name and profile information
  • Firebase user identifier
  • Authentication and account-security information
  • Technical information required by Firebase to provide authentication and protect the service, such as IP address and user-agent information

Email/password authentication is not part of the current Nitro Driver release profile.

Passenger and journey information

When you scan a passenger QR code, Nitro may process:

  • Passenger name
  • Passenger address
  • Passenger latitude and longitude
  • Journey origin and route coordinates
  • Passenger drop-off status
  • Active journey information

This information is used only to support the driver’s journey inside Nitro.

Camera

Nitro uses the device camera to scan passenger QR codes. Nitro does not intentionally store or upload camera images.

Device location

Nitro may use device location when you choose the My location function to help set your journey origin.

Nitro does not use background location tracking.

Local device storage

Nitro stores the active journey and saved origin locally on the device. This is done using device storage and is not stored in a Nitro backend database.

3. How Nitro uses information

Nitro uses information to:

  • Authenticate the driver
  • Maintain the signed-in session
  • Process passenger QR codes
  • Display passenger and journey information
  • Save and restore the active local journey
  • Set and use the driver’s origin
  • Optimise routes
  • Display maps and navigation information
  • Handle authentication, security, and network failures
  • Process account-deletion requests

Nitro does not sell personal information and does not use the current release for advertising, analytics, or targeted marketing.

4. Third-party services

Nitro uses the following services.

Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In

Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In are used to authenticate drivers and manage Nitro accounts.

Google Maps Platform and Google Maps

Nitro uses Google Maps services to:

  • Display maps
  • Calculate and optimise routes
  • Support external navigation

When these features are used, route and location coordinates may be sent to Google Maps services. Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and service terms.

Firebase explains that Firebase customers generally control the data they provide to Firebase, while Google generally processes that data on the customer’s behalf. Firebase Authentication may process account details, IP addresses, and user-agent information for authentication and security. See Firebase privacy information.

5. Data storage and retention

  • Active journey and origin data are stored locally on the device.
  • Local journey data may remain until the journey is finished, cleared, deleted through account deletion, or removed by clearing the app’s storage.
  • The saved origin is reset after successful account deletion.
  • Nitro currently has no backend database containing passenger, journey, or organisation records.
  • Firebase account information remains until the account is successfully deleted.
  • Firebase or other service providers may retain limited security logs or backup information according to their applicable retention practices and legal obligations.

If Nitro introduces backend services or additional data collection in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.

6. Account and data deletion

You can delete your Nitro Driver account:

  1. Inside Nitro through Account → Delete account; or
  2. Through the external deletion webpage:
    https://nitro-ecosystem.github.io/driver/delete-account/index.html

When deletion succeeds, Nitro:

  • Deletes the Firebase Authentication account
  • Clears local journey data
  • Resets the saved origin
  • Signs the driver out
  • Prevents continued access to the deleted Firebase account

Nitro currently has no server-side passenger or journey records to delete.

Signing in with Google again after deletion creates a new Firebase account. It does not restore the deleted account.

Google Play requires a privacy policy to be accessible inside the app and linked in Play Console. Apps that allow account creation must also provide in-app and external account-deletion pathways. See Google Play User Data policy and Google account-deletion guidance.

7. Data security

Nitro uses Firebase Authentication and HTTPS-based services to protect information in transit.

Local journey information is stored on the device. Nitro does not include Firebase Admin credentials or other privileged backend credentials in the mobile app or public webpage.
